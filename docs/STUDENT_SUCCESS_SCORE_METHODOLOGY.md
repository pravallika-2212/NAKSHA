# ASCENDIQ: Student Success Score Methodology Note
*Version 2.0 · Comprehensive Human-in-the-Loop Academic Intelligence Framework*

---

## 1. Purpose & Guiding Principles

The **ASCENDIQ Student Success Score** is a transparent, explainable 0–100 progress indicator designed to synthesize multi-dimensional campus signals into actionable insight for students, mentors, and placement teams.

### Core Ethical Guidelines:
1. **Explainable by Design:** The score is never generated by an uninterpretable black box or ungrounded machine learning model. Every point is traceable to explicit mathematical contributions and verified records.
2. **Supportive, Not Punitive:** A lower score is treated as an invitation for proactive advising, peer tutoring, and skill development—never as a label of student capability or permanent potential.
3. **Missing-Data Fairness:** Missing records are **never silently imputed as zero**. When certain data categories are pending upload or unavailable, their weights are proportionally re-normalized across available categories.
4. **Independent from Risk:** Risk is **not** defined simply as `100 - Success Score`. ASCENDIQ calculates a distinct, rule-based **Risk Score** driven by explicit threshold breaches (e.g., attendance < 75%, active backlogs, sharp semester drops).

## 2. Component Indicators & Mathematical Normalization

All raw metrics are normalized onto a consistent 0–100 scale:

| Indicator Group | Raw Metrics Tracked | Normalization Formula | Minimum Reliable Range |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Academic Performance** | Semester Marks %, CGPA (10.0 scale), Active Backlogs | `Score = Max(0, Min(100, (Marks% || (CGPA*10)) - (Backlogs * 5)))` | Passing cutoff: 50% |
| **Attendance Discipline** | Classes Held, Classes Attended, Biometric Logs | `Score = (Classes Attended / Classes Held) * 100` | Mandatory cutoff: 75% |
| **LMS Learning Activity** | Assignment Completion %, Active Minutes/Week, Overdue Submissions | `Combined = (0.70 * Completion%) + (0.30 * Min(100, Minutes/120 * 100)) - (Overdue * 5)` | Target: ≥ 80% completion |
| **Placement Readiness** | Coding Assessment %, Aptitude %, Mock Interview Rubric, Tech/Soft Score | `Score = Average(Available Assessment Percentage Scores)` | Tier-1 Target: Coding ≥ 80%, Aptitude ≥ 70% |
| **Extracurricular Engagement** | Events, Student Clubs, Hackathons, Verified Certifications | `Score = Min(100, (Events*15) + (Clubs*20) + (Hackathons*35) + (Certs*30))` | Cap: 100 points |
| **Technical & Soft Skills** | Domain Technical Tests, Communication & HR Rubrics | `Score = Average(Verified Assessment Scores)` | Benchmark: ≥ 70% |
| **Faculty & Course Feedback** | Course Satisfaction Rating, Mentor Rubric Score | `Score = (Faculty Rating + Satisfaction Score) / 2` | Range: 0–100 |

## 3. Progressive Academic Year Weight Profiles

The priority of university life evolves across the four academic years. ASCENDIQ dynamically adapts the weighting profiles to mirror institutional objectives:

| Indicator Group | Year 1: Foundation & Adjustment | Year 2: Skill Development | Year 3: Career Preparation | Year 4: Placement Acceleration |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **Academic Performance** | **40%** | **35%** | **30%** | **25%** |
| **Attendance Discipline** | **25%** | **20%** | **15%** | **15%** |
| **LMS Learning Consistency** | **20%** | **15%** | **10%** | **5%** |
| **Placement Readiness** | **0%** *(Awareness only)* | **5%** *(Diagnostic)* | **20%** | **30%** *(Primary focus)* |
| **Technical & Soft Skills** | **5%** | **15%** | **15%** | **15%** |
| **Extracurricular Engagement** | **10%** | **10%** | **5%** | **5%** |
| **Faculty / Course Feedback** | — | — | **5%** | **5%** |
| **Total Weight** | **100%** | **100%** | **100%** | **100%** |

### Rationale for Year-Specific Weighting:
- **Year 1:** Helping students adapt to university coursework, build classroom attendance discipline, and avoid backlogs is paramount. Placement readiness is de-emphasized.
- **Year 2:** Emphasis shifts toward practical competence: mini-projects, hackathons, and certifications.
- **Year 3:** Career preparation emerges: diagnostic aptitude tests, algorithmic problem-solving, and mock technical interviews.
- **Year 4:** Placement execution becomes central: meeting corporate cutoffs, aptitude readiness, coding rounds, and institutional sign-offs.

## 4. Explainable Aggregation & Re-normalization Formula

Let $W_i$ be the configured weight for indicator $i \in \{1, \dots, K\}$, and $S_i$ be the normalized observed score.

### Standard Calculation (Full Coverage):
$$\text{Success Score} = \sum_{i=1}^{K} \left( S_i \times \frac{W_i}{100} \right)$$

### Re-normalization Policy (Missing Data):
If a subset of indicators $M \subset \{1, \dots, K\}$ is missing:
1. Total available weight is calculated: $W_{\text{avail}} = \sum_{j \notin M} W_j$
2. If data coverage is below **60%**, the score status is designated as **`insufficient_data`** and withheld to prevent misleading conclusions.
3. If data coverage is between **60% and 99%**, the score status is designated as **`provisional`**, and weights are re-normalized:
$$\text{Provisional Score} = \sum_{j \notin M} \left( S_j \times \frac{W_j}{W_{\text{avail}}} \right)$$

## 5. Early Warning Risk Signal Engine

The Risk Score (0–100) operates independently from the Success Score and triggers explainable advising alerts:

| Trigger Signal | Configured Threshold | Point Penalty | Advising Intervention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Critical Attendance Shortage** | Attendance < 65% | +35 pts | Class advisor advising & parent conference |
| **Moderate Attendance Warning** | Attendance < 75% | +20 pts | Attendance recovery agreement |
| **Active Academic Backlogs** | Backlogs ≥ 2 | +25 pts | Mandatory tutorial hours & supplementary exam registration |
| **Semester Grade Drop** | Performance drop ≥ 10% | +20 pts | Remedial subject tutoring & concept revision |
| **LMS Assignment Neglect** | Overdue assignments ≥ 2 | +20 pts | Coursework catch-up schedule |
| **Placement Coding Gap** | Coding assessment < 50% | +20 pts | Weekly algorithmic problem-solving labs |
| **Placement Aptitude Gap** | Aptitude assessment < 50% | +15 pts | Quantitative reasoning prep modules |

### Risk Categories:
- **Low Concern (0–24 pts):** On-track trajectory.
- **Moderate (25–49 pts):** Emerging gaps requiring student attention.
- **High Priority (50–74 pts):** Coordinated mentor intervention recommended.
- **Critical (75–100 pts):** Severe multi-dimensional bottlenecks requiring department intervention.
